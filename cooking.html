Beverage

Right now Lera knows how to make lattes and milk tea.

Instructions:

Lattes:

  1. pour coffee concentrate into a cup (since Lera doesn't have coffee beans or a coffee machine).
  2. whisk it with a frother until it's rich and creamy.
  3. pour in milk and ice.

Milk tea:

  1. Heat a small pot first, and once it's hot, add the tea leaves.
  2. Add a small spoonful of white sugar (adjust to your taste. This recipe makes lightly sweetened milk tea). Once the sugar melts, stir-fry it together with the tea leaves.
  3. Add an appropriate amount of water to the pot to make a pot of tea broth.
  4. Once the tea broth comes to a boil, add the milk.
  5. Turn the heat to low and wait for the milk to come to a boil.
